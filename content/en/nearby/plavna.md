Plavna is a mixed village of Bač municipality, Hungarian *Palona*, German *Plawingen*, with the Catholic church of St James ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plavna)).

In the woods near the village is the **Plavna hunting ground** with a hunting lodge: besides hunting it offers guided tours, photo safaris and wildlife watching. Deer, wild boar, hares, pheasants, partridges and wild ducks live on some 600 ha of fields ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plavna)).
