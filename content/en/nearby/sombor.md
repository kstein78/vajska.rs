Sombor is the most interesting town nearby, about an hour and twenty minutes by car. It is known as one of the greenest cities in Serbia and became a free royal city in 1749 ([Vojvodina Travel](https://vojvodina.travel/en/destinations/sombor/)).

The Great Hall of the old County Hall holds Ferenc Eisenhut's **"Battle of Senta"**: oil on a single canvas of about 4 × 7 m, some 40 m² with the frame, the largest painting on canvas in Serbia ([Telegraf](https://www.telegraf.rs/pop-i-kultura/art/3605082-najveca-slika-u-srbiji-nalazi-se-u-somboru), [Vojvodina Travel](https://vojvodina.travel/en/destinations/sombor/)). The town also has the Milan Konjović Gallery and the "Laza Kostić" gallery, and to the north lies the **Gornje Podunavlje** reserve ([Vojvodina Travel](https://vojvodina.travel/en/destinations/sombor/)).
