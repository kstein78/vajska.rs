Lake Provala lies about a kilometre from the village, towards Bođani. It formed when the Danube broke through an embankment in a great flood; sources give 1924 or 1926. Its greatest depth is about 19 m, which is why it is called the deepest lake in Vojvodina ([Vojvodina Travel](https://vojvodina.travel/en/experiences/lake-provala-vajska/)). On 21 May 2024 Vajska celebrated the lake's hundredth year ([Dnevnik](https://www.dnevnik.rs/vojvodina/foto-dnevnik-u-selu-kraj-vode-vajska-prirodno-jezero-provala-na-ponos-svih-mestana-22-04)).

In summer there is a sandy beach with parasols, changing cabins and showers, a campsite and a restaurant. There is no lifeguard; you swim at your own risk ([Vojvodina Travel](https://vojvodina.travel/en/experiences/lake-provala-vajska/)).

Everything about swimming, fishing, places to stay and the lake's nature is on our sister site **[provala.rs](https://provala.rs/en/)**.
