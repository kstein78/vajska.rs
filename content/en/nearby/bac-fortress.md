The present fortress in Bač was built between 1338 and 1342, in the reign of the Hungarian king Charles Robert of Anjou. It has four side towers and a central keep (donjon) 22 metres high ([Vojvodina Travel](https://vojvodina.travel/bac-fortress-bac)). It is considered the best-preserved medieval fort in Vojvodina ([Wikipedia: Bač](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ba%C4%8D,_Serbia)).

The fortress hosts **Medieval Day** in summer and, in September, **European Heritage Days – Days of Bač** ([Bač Tourist Organisation](https://turizambac.rs/najave-dogadaja/)). Opening hours and tickets: Bač Tourist Organisation, +381 21 60 72 222 ([Vojvodina Travel](https://vojvodina.travel/bac-fortress-bac)).
