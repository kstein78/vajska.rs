## Who is behind the site

- **[Svetlana Morozova]({{< relref "author" >}})**, editor and the face of the site. She lives in Vajska, has been a journalist since 1999 and writes books on the cuisine of southern Russia.
- **Konstantin (Kosta) Stein**, co-founder: creator and initiator of the network, builds and develops the sites. He lives in Rakovac on Fruška Gora and made [rakovac.rs](https://rakovac.rs/en/).

## One network, different sites

Each site of the network is about one place and speaks to its own readers. **vajska.rs** is the village's site: for residents and for people who want to live in Vajska and explore the region from there. **provala.rs** is the lake's site: holidays, swimming, fishing and nature. The sites share one look and one engine and link to each other wherever that helps the reader.

## How we work

- Facts are checked against sources, listed at the end of each page. Anything unconfirmed is marked as such.
- Photos: until we take our own, the site shows illustrations and marked photo spaces. Photos by others appear only with permission and always with the author's name.
- Texts are licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
