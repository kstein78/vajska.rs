**Thursday is market day in Vajska.** The village centre is busy then and quiets down quickly once the stalls are packed away ([Dnevnik](https://www.dnevnik.rs/vojvodina/foto-dnevnik-u-selu-kraj-vode-vajska-prirodno-jezero-provala-na-ponos-svih-mestana-22-04)). The stalls stand under metal shelters and along the street, and the sellers' pitches are numbered in chalk on the asphalt.

## What is sold

- **Vegetables and fruit**: cabbage, cauliflower, celeriac, parsnips, carrots, potatoes and onions; apples and, in autumn, grapes.
- **Smoked meats and cheese**: sausages, bacon and ham, cheese in tubs.
- **For the pantry**: several kinds of beans, hazelnuts, walnuts, poppy seed, ground paprika, homemade noodles.
- **Clothes and shoes** on racks.
- **Flowers**: before the days when families visit the cemeteries, arrangements of artificial flowers on fir branches.

## Good to know

- Bring **cash**, ideally small notes.
- Bring your own **bag** or trolley.
- What is on offer depends on the season and on who has come that Thursday.
