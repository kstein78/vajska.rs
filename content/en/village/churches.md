## St George's church

The **Roman Catholic church of St George** stands in the village centre ([Dnevnik](https://www.dnevnik.rs/vojvodina/foto-dnevnik-u-selu-kraj-vode-vajska-prirodno-jezero-provala-na-ponos-svih-mestana-22-04)). On St George's Day in the Catholic calendar, **23 April**, Vajska celebrates its *kirbaj*, the village feast ([Vreme, 2020](https://vreme.com/mozaik/za-kim-zvone-backa-zvona/)).

## The church of St Petka

Next to the [memorial fountain of the Salonika volunteers]({{< relref "village/solunci" >}}) and the park, an **Orthodox church dedicated to St Petka (St Paraskevi)** is being built. In April 2024 the work had been going on for about five years, paid for entirely by voluntary donations. The bells were hung in 2023, the money for the crosses had been raised, and what was left over from building the fountain went to the church's three doors. Villagers then expected services to begin in about two years ([Dnevnik](https://www.dnevnik.rs/vojvodina/foto-dnevnik-u-selu-kraj-vode-vajska-prirodno-jezero-provala-na-ponos-svih-mestana-22-04)).

{{< photo key="petkovdan-church-crowd" >}}

On **Petkovdan, 27 October 2026**, the church celebrated its feast day: priests and villagers welcomed the bishop with bread and flowers, and the villagers filled the church. Photos and video: [St Petka's Day 2026 in Vajska]({{< relref "gallery/petkovdan-2026" >}}).
