Since **July 2019** Vajska has had its **Šokačka kuća** (Šokac house), the home of the Croatian cultural society **“Antun Sorgg”**. The society bought it with support from Croatia's Vukovar-Srijem County and the Croatian National Council, at the initiative of the council's vice-president Željko Pakledinac. It is a meeting place, a heritage house and a stage for the Šokci, the Catholic Croats of this part of the Danube ([ZKVH](https://www.zkvh.org.rs/etno-kuce-post/etno-kuce/sokacka-kuca-vajska)).

## The house

The house was in fairly good condition, so not a single wall was pulled down. Evica Bartulov from Plavna re-plastered the walls the traditional way. There are two rooms, a kitchen and a small room meant to become a library; the "best" room (*pridnja* or *čista soba*) has beds with feather bedding and crocheted pillowcases. Most of the furniture and objects were given by the Šimunović family of Vajska and the Kovačev family of Bođani, and most were only cleaned and treated with linseed oil to keep them authentic ([ZKVH](https://www.zkvh.org.rs/etno-kuce-post/etno-kuce/sokacka-kuca-vajska)).

## What you can see

- **Costumes over a hundred years old** from Bač, Vajska, Plavna and Bođani, and one from Subotica from the 1930s–40s, with sequins, embroidery and crocheted lace on sleeves and skirts.
- Hand-woven **dereklijani** cloth, skirts, waistcoats and embroidered bedspreads, rag rugs and rugs from the loom.
- **Distaffs, a reed and a loom**, kneading troughs, lard pots, bread baskets, old plates, irons, a radio and a **chest from 1884**, holy pictures and dressers ([ZKVH](https://www.zkvh.org.rs/etno-kuce-post/etno-kuce/sokacka-kuca-vajska)).

## “Vridne ruke” and plans

The house is home to the **“Vridne ruke”** (“busy hands”) group of about ten women, who cook and bake from old family recipes and do embroidery and handicrafts. In the yard there is a summer kitchen, and the old room for drying and processing paprika still has its oven; an exhibition space is planned there. There were also plans for a few bungalows for about ten guests and for offering home-made food together with the Bač tourist board ([ZKVH](https://www.zkvh.org.rs/etno-kuce-post/etno-kuce/sokacka-kuca-vajska)). These details are from 2021; we will check what has been done since.

## Village homeland day

In early July the Šokac house hosts Vajska's **Zavitno-zavičajni dan**, a votive and homeland day ([ZKVH](https://www.zkvh.org.rs/etno-kuce-post/etno-kuce/sokacka-kuca-vajska)). We post the date in the [news]({{< relref "news" >}}).
