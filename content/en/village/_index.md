## The village

Vajska is in Bač municipality, South Bačka District ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vajska)). According to municipal data it is an ethnically mixed village with a relative Serb majority ([Wikipedia: Bač](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ba%C4%8D,_Serbia)). In Hungarian it is called *Vajszka* or *Józsefháza*, in German *Wajska* ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vajska)).

Two separate settlements officially belong to the village: **Labudnjača** (from *labud*, "swan") and **Živa** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vajska)).

## The church of St George and the kirbaj

The Catholic church of **St George** stands in the centre of the village. The kirbaj, the village feast, is held on **23 April**, St George's Day in the Catholic calendar ([Vreme, 2020](https://vreme.com/mozaik/za-kim-zvone-backa-zvona/)).
