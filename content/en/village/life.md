Vajska has about **2,000 inhabitants** of some twenty nationalities, and relations between them are excellent, says **Momir Svorcan**, secretary of the local community ([Dnevnik](https://www.dnevnik.rs/vojvodina/foto-dnevnik-u-selu-kraj-vode-vajska-prirodno-jezero-provala-na-ponos-svih-mestana-22-04)). These figures are from April 2024.

## Thursday market

**Thursday is market day.** The village centre is busy then and quiets down quickly once the stalls are packed away ([Dnevnik](https://www.dnevnik.rs/vojvodina/foto-dnevnik-u-selu-kraj-vode-vajska-prirodno-jezero-provala-na-ponos-svih-mestana-22-04)). What is sold and what to know: [Thursday market in Vajska]({{< relref "village/market" >}}).

## What the village lives on

Most people live from **farming**, as the soil is good: mostly **maize, soya and wheat**. Younger farmers increasingly grow vegetables too, because dry-land arable farming barely pays ([Dnevnik](https://www.dnevnik.rs/vojvodina/foto-dnevnik-u-selu-kraj-vode-vajska-prirodno-jezero-provala-na-ponos-svih-mestana-22-04)). **Lajoš Bođo** and his wife, for example, grow peppers, tomatoes, cucumbers and potatoes on about 2.5 hectares, in a polytunnel and in the open, as well as sweet and hot paprika for spice. They sell mainly at the markets in Vajska and Bač ([Dnevnik](https://www.dnevnik.rs/vojvodina/foto-dnevnik-u-selu-kraj-vode-vajska-prirodno-jezero-provala-na-ponos-svih-mestana-22-04)).

## Clubs and societies

- **The St Sava cultural society** (*KUD „Sveti Sava”*): its literary and music section has been going for about ten years. It was started by **Blaženka Davidović**, who has worked as a kindergarten teacher in the village for 35 years. About thirty children take part, and older villagers join the performances. It works on history, tradition, the First World War, the [Salonika volunteers]({{< relref "village/solunci" >}}) and colonisation, and cooperates with every society in the municipality of Bač ([Dnevnik](https://www.dnevnik.rs/vojvodina/foto-dnevnik-u-selu-kraj-vode-vajska-prirodno-jezero-provala-na-ponos-svih-mestana-22-04)).
- **The Šaran fishing club, Vajska** stocks and looks after [Lake Provala](https://provala.rs/en/). Blaženka and Miodrag “Dragan” Davidović were Serbia's angling champions in 1999 and 2000 and twice runners-up; Blaženka is a federal angling judge. The municipality of Bač gave them two October Awards: for the Šaran club and for their work with the St Sava society ([Dnevnik](https://www.dnevnik.rs/vojvodina/foto-dnevnik-u-selu-kraj-vode-vajska-prirodno-jezero-provala-na-ponos-svih-mestana-22-04)).
- **The volunteer fire brigade**: village celebrations and meetings are held in its hall, which is in poor condition. What the village wants most is a new place for celebrations and gatherings ([Dnevnik](https://www.dnevnik.rs/vojvodina/foto-dnevnik-u-selu-kraj-vode-vajska-prirodno-jezero-provala-na-ponos-svih-mestana-22-04)).

## What has changed

In 2023 several streets were asphalted and the village centre was improved; more work of this kind is planned ([Dnevnik](https://www.dnevnik.rs/vojvodina/foto-dnevnik-u-selu-kraj-vode-vajska-prirodno-jezero-provala-na-ponos-svih-mestana-22-04)).
