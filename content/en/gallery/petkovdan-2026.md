On Petkovdan, 27 October 2026, the Orthodox [church of St Petka]({{< relref "village/churches" >}}) in Vajska celebrated its feast day. Many villagers gathered in front of the church; priests and girls in Serbian folk costume welcomed the bishop with bread and flowers, and a white carpet led to the church door. Homemade pastries waited under the shelter.

{{< localvideo src="video/vajska-petkovdan-2026.mp4" poster="img/vajska-petkovdan-2026-poster.jpg" title="The bishop and priests walk to the church of St Petka, Petkovdan 2026" author="Svetlana Morozova" >}}

Next to the church stands the [memorial fountain of the Salonika volunteers]({{< relref "village/solunci" >}}), with the plaque "Potomci precima" (descendants to their ancestors) and their names.
