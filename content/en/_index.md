## Where Vajska is

Vajska is a village in Bač municipality, South Bačka District, near the Danube ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vajska)). Its territory also includes the separate settlements of **Labudnjača** and **Živa**. Bač is about 10 km away, Novi Sad about 75 km by car.

## For residents and visitors

We make this site for the people of Vajska and for those who want to come: what is happening in the village, whom to contact, when the feasts are and what to see nearby. For a holiday at the lake there is a separate site, [provala.rs](https://provala.rs/en/).
