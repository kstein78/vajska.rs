**Svetlana Vadimovna Morozova** is the face and editor of **vajska.rs** and **provala.rs**. She lives in Vajska.

## Journalist and editor

A historian by training, she has worked in journalism since 1999 ([BLITZ.plus](https://blitz.plus/svetlana-morozova)). For many years she edited online media, so a local site where every fact is checked is a natural continuation of that work.

## Books on the cuisine of southern Russia

Svetlana is the author of the series "A Culinary Journey through Southern Russia" (Russian: *Кулинарное путешествие по югу России*, published by IM Media, Moscow):

- **Taganrog. Stories and recipes** (2021): the multinational cooking of Taganrog, family recipes of its residents and the history of the city that nearly became Russia's capital ([LiveLib](https://www.livelib.ru/book/1007026369-kulinarnoe-puteshestvie-po-yugu-rossii-taganrog-istorii-i-retsepty-svetlana-morozova));
- **Krasnodar. Stanitsa food of a southern metropolis** (2023): the cooking of the Kuban stanitsas and why Kuban borscht is made without beetroot ([Chitai-gorod](https://www.chitai-gorod.ru/product/kulinarnoe-puteshestvie-po-yugu-rossii-krasnodar-stanichnaya-eda-yuzhnogo-megapolisa-2979531));
- **Rostov-on-Don. Old districts and unusual recipes**;
- the combined edition **Taganrog, Rostov-on-Don, Krasnodar. Stories and recipes of southern Russia** ([LitRes](https://www.litres.ru/author/svetlana-morozova-31890639/)).

She loves to cook and collects cookery books. That is why the site has a food section too: what is cooked in Vajska and Bačka, and what of it you can try at home.
