There is a new page, [Maps of Vajska]({{< relref "village/maps" >}}). It shows the Yugoslav People's Army topographic maps at 1:25,000, 1:100,000 and 1:200,000 from the 1970s, published with the permission of Haxhi Berisha, the owner of [topografskakarta.com](https://www.topografskakarta.com/), and two Austro-Hungarian maps of 1910 and 1914 drawn before Lake Provala existed.
