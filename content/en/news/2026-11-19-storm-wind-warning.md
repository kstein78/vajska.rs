**Update, 19 November, 16:00:** according to RHMZ meteorologist Slobodan Sovilj, the wind will keep strengthening during the day, with the strongest gusts expected **between 21:00 and midnight**. Overnight into Friday the rain stops, the sky clears and the wind weakens ([Danas](https://www.danas.rs/vesti/drustvo/meteorolog-slobodan-sovilj-rhmz-vetar-oluja/)).

On Thursday, 19 November, at 9:10 the Republic Hydrometeorological Service of Serbia (RHMZ) issued an urgent weather warning and sent emergency SMS messages to people. **This evening**, as a cold front passes, **storm and hurricane-force gusts of westerly and north-westerly wind of 80 to 110 km/h** are expected in Vojvodina, Belgrade and western and central Serbia.

The **red** alert ("particularly dangerous weather that can cause great material damage") covers Belgrade, Srem, Banat and **Bačka**. Vajska lies on the open plain by the Danube, where nothing stops a westerly wind, and the poplars and willows of the riverside forests, along the embankment and around Provala break and fall easily.

The RHMZ forecast says it turns warm again on Friday (16–20 °C), and from Saturday temperatures will gradually fall, with occasional rain.

## Before evening: house and yard

- **Bring in or tie down everything the wind can pick up**: garden furniture, flower pots, bins, tarpaulins, sheets of tin, ladders, boards. Close greenhouses and the doors of sheds, stables and barns.
- **Close windows and shutters**, fix the gates. Pull the curtains: they hold back the glass if a pane breaks.
- **Move cars and machinery** away from trees, poles and roofs, preferably into a garage or the open.
- **Do not burn leaves or stubble**, and check the stove and chimney: in strong wind a spark carries far.
- **Bring animals in**, secure the hives.

## On the Danube and Provala

- **Do not go out on the Danube** or fishing tonight. Pull boats on the Danube ashore or double their moorings, and put the gear away.
- Owners of weekend houses at Provala and by the Danube: shut and secure everything before dark, and do not stay under trees.

## If the power goes

- **Charge phones, power banks** and battery lamps; have a **torch, candles and matches** ready.
- **Store water**: without electricity, pumps and hydrophores stop.
- Get **warm blankets** and **food that needs no cooking** ready.

## During the storm

- **Stay indoors** and away from windows. Do not set off unless you must, especially on the road through the forest and along the embankment.
- **Do not stand under trees** or poles, **do not touch fallen wires**, and report them.
- Check on older neighbours who live alone.

## Numbers

- **112** — emergency services; **192** — police, **193** — firefighters, **194** — ambulance
- **Power cuts and fallen wires**: Elektrodistribucija Srbije, free number [0800 360 300](tel:0800360300)
