Based on Dnevnik's report “Dnevnik in the village by the water – Vajska” (April 2024) we have extended the [history of the village]({{< relref "village/history" >}}) and added new pages: [The Salonika volunteers of Vajska]({{< relref "village/solunci" >}}), [Churches of Vajska]({{< relref "village/churches" >}}) and [Life in Vajska]({{< relref "village/life" >}}). The memorial service for the Salonika volunteers on 24 May is now in the [calendar]({{< relref "events" >}}).

There is also a new page on [the Šokac house in Vajska]({{< relref "village/sokacka-kuca" >}}), based on an article by the [Institute for the Culture of Vojvodina Croats](https://www.zkvh.org.rs/etno-kuce-post/etno-kuce/sokacka-kuca-vajska).
